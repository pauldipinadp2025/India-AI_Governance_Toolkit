Method and limits

The rule engine encodes obligations directly from the statutory text and the 2025 Rules. Where the law is silent or pending notification, the tool states this in its output rather than inferring a position. Scoring weights reflect the author's judgement, calibrated against the penalty schedule, and each deduction is displayed alongside its finding so the reasoning can be examined and disputed.

Rules 1 to 16 and the Schedules were read directly from gazette notification G.S.R. 846(E) on 8 September 2026. The penalty caps were read from the Schedule to section 33(1) of the Act as published on meity.gov.in. The framework remains mid-transition, with full obligations in force from May 2027, so findings reflect the position at the review date rather than necessarily the position today.
